Questions

Things people ask before they start

How much does a website cost?

A landing page is usually R2,000 to R4,000 and a full business website is usually R4,800 to R9,000. You get one fixed price in writing before anything starts, so there is no bill at the end that you were not expecting.

How long does it take?

Seven to ten days from the day I have your information and photos. You see the site before it goes live and we change anything you are not happy with.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours. If you ever decide to move to someone else, it goes with you. No lock-in. The first year of the domain is included; from year two it renews at R250 per year, which covers the registration and me handling the renewal so it never lapses.

Will I show up when someone searches for a salon near me?

I make sure you are eligible: your Google Business Profile is set up with the right category, your name, address and phone number are identical everywhere online, your website tells Google exactly what you do and where you are, and there are buttons that let a customer call or WhatsApp you in one tap.

How high you come up then depends on how close the person searching is, how many reviews you have and how many competitors are around you. Nobody can promise number one, and I won't. What I will do is send you a report every month so you can see what is moving.

What happens after the site goes live?

Small fixes are free for the first 30 days. After that there are two care plans. Website care at R350 a month covers hosting, backups, a monthly check-up, 30 minutes of changes and a monthly report. Website care+ at R650 a month adds a monthly Google post, a review push to your recent customers and a ranking report. Both can be cancelled any time.

Do I need to pay everything upfront?

No. A 50% deposit books your slot and starts the work, and the balance is due just before the site goes live.
