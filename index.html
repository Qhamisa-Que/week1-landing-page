About Rhadebe Digital

Rhadebe Digital is a small web design studio based in Mthatha, Eastern Cape, founded by Qhamisa Mnamatha. We build clean, fast, mobile-friendly websites for guesthouses, salons, contractors and other local businesses — the kind of site that loads quickly on a phone, shows up on Google, and makes it easy for customers to call or WhatsApp you. Every site is hand-coded, not dragged and dropped, so it stays fast and cheap to run.
