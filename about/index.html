Background

Web design is the work I do now. The IT support and cybersecurity training is what makes the result solid.

  • National Diploma in Information Technology, NQF Level 6 — Berea College of Technology, 2022 to 2025
  • IT in-service trainee at DKSN Business Solution, Mthatha, April to September 2025
  • Desktop and laptop support: finding and fixing hardware and software faults, and helping the people using them
  • Installing, configuring and upgrading computers, including Windows setup and system recovery
  • Network, Wi-Fi and printer support
  • Cisco Networking Academy certificate, Introduction to Cybersecurity, July 2026
